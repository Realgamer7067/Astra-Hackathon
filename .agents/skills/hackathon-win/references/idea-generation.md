# Idea Generation

Use only when the idea is not locked or when the current idea fails a serious pressure test.

## Diverge before selecting

Research the problem statements, previous winners, obvious products, current tools, usable data/APIs, sponsor tracks, and team constraints. With adequate time, generate 12-20 distinct concepts before ranking; under six hours, compare 2-4 tightly scoped options. Do not run a tournament for an already locked idea unless the user requests it or concrete feasibility/eligibility evidence warrants reconsideration.

Vary the advantage, not merely the industry label:

- practical utility and automation;
- technical novelty or unusual integration;
- emotional or social impact;
- visual spectacle or interactive data;
- accessibility and education;
- sustainability and community outcomes;
- developer tools and productivity;
- AI, multimodal, realtime, edge, hardware, or optimization where genuinely useful;
- sponsor technology that naturally strengthens the product.

Avoid twenty versions of "chat with your X." Each candidate must specify:

- real user and painful moment;
- one-sentence outcome;
- central technical claim;
- 60-90 second demoable workflow;
- potential wow moment;
- required dependencies/data;
- why this team can finish it.

## Four-layer reduction

Reduce viable candidates to:

1. **CORE STORY:** one sentence explaining who cares and why.
2. **MVP:** smallest end-to-end product proving the claim.
3. **WOW FEATURE:** one specific presentation second intended to trigger excitement.
4. **STRETCH FEATURES:** only after MVP and demo stability.

Default target: `1 strong story + 1 reliable workflow + 1 memorable wow moment`, not fifteen incomplete features.

## AI necessity check

If AI is used, answer:

- Why is AI necessary for this task?
- What nontrivial system exists around the model?
- What unique data, workflow, orchestration, evaluation, guardrail, or UX differentiates it?
- What part genuinely works beyond a prompt and a text box?

If the honest description is "ChatGPT but for X," redesign or reject it. Useful differentiation can come from workflow depth, proprietary or hard-to-use data, multimodal interaction, closed-loop action, verification, collaboration, or a remarkable interface—not from claiming a model as the product.

## Stop condition

Move to the tournament once additional candidates are variations rather than new strategic options. Do not keep ideating to avoid committing.
