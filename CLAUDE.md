## Shared skill catalog

Installed skill snapshots and supporting files are committed under `.agents/skills/`; read `.agents/skills/README.md` to select a relevant skill and open its `SKILL.md` explicitly if native discovery is unavailable. Repository and user instructions take precedence. Availability of a skill does not imply its required tools or services are installed.
